# Slide 1 — What Is OAA?

**Title:** Organizational Agent Architecture (OAA)
**Tagline:** Structure your agents like you structure your company.

---

## Key Message

OAA is a file-and-directory convention for organizing multi-agent systems using the same structure companies use: agents fill roles, roles require skills, skills require tools. Authority (who owns what, what they can decide autonomously, what they must escalate, what is absolutely forbidden) is declared in a machine-readable block that composes automatically down the chain.

## Speaker Notes

- OAA is NOT a protocol or a runtime — it is a convention. Adoptable with a text editor.
- The analogy to a company org chart is literal: roles own domains, agents fill roles, authority flows downward.
- The key innovation is making authority machine-readable and checkable, not just prose in a README.
- The convention is host-agnostic — works with Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, any MCP-compatible host.

## Why This Slide Exists

The audience may know what MCP and agent skills are. This slide establishes that OAA lives above those layers — it gives the organizational structure that makes agents responsible rather than reckless.
